# TikTok ad copy — In-Feed

The copy has to read like a real post, not an ad, and it sits *over* the playing video — so it competes
with TikTok's own interface for space.

## The fields you fill

- **Ad text / caption** — the line(s) over the bottom-left of the video.
- **Display name** — the account/brand nickname by the profile photo.

(Spark Ads pull the caption from the organic post instead of writing it in Ads Manager.)

## Limits and the fold

| Field | Budget | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caption (non-Spark) | **≤100 characters** recommended (≤50 for CJK) | shows max **4 lines**, then "See more" |
| Caption (Spark Pull) | up to 150 characters, or blank | pulled from the post |
| Display name | **≤20 characters** hard (≤10 CJK), 4–10 recommended | one line only |

Keep the caption within ~100 characters so it isn't hidden behind "See more"; front-load the hook and
the CTA into the first line or two. Short and punchy wins here — the video carries the message. Verify
current limits in Ads Manager; the caption number is a fold-driven recommendation rather than a hard cap.

## The safe zone — critical on TikTok

The caption sits over the live video and shares the screen with TikTok's own UI: the **right-side
action rail** (profile, like, comment, share, sound) and the **bottom caption bar**. Elements outside
the safe zone get covered or cropped, and a **longer caption shrinks the usable safe zone**. Keep the
must-read words clear of where the UI sits — another reason to keep it short.

## Calls to action

A large preset set (around 25), varying by objective — Shop Now, Learn More, Sign Up, Download, Install
Now, Order Now, Book Now, Watch Now, Pre-order Now, and more. Pick the real next step.

## Register and craft

Native and non-ad-like: conversational, first-person, trend-aware, the way a creator posts, not a brand
banner. Front-load the hook. Emoji are allowed in the caption (but **not** in the account name, and
`{ }` isn't allowed in names). In non-Spark ads, `@`mentions and hashtags render but aren't clickable,
so don't rely on them as links. The caption font is a fixed white typeface you can't style.

## Before handing back

- Caption ≤~100 characters, hook and CTA in the first line or two, must-read words clear of the UI rail.
- Reads like an organic post, not an ad.
- Display name ≤20 characters, no emoji in it.
- The CTA names the real next step.
