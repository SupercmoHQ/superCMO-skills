# LinkedIn ad copy — Sponsored Content (single image)

A professional, B2B context. Lead with a business outcome, and get the value in before the fold.

## The fields you fill

- **Introductory text** — the post body above the image. The hook and the value proposition.
- **Headline** — the bold line in the banner below the image.
- **Description** — shows only on the LinkedIn Audience Network / off-feed placements, not the native
  feed. Keep it able to stand alone, but treat it as secondary.
- **Ad name** — internal only, never shown to members. No member-facing copy here.

## Limits and the fold

LinkedIn publishes a **recommended** length (to avoid truncation) and a much larger **hard max**. Write
to the recommendation; the ceiling is a ceiling, not a target.

| Field | Recommended (target) | Hard max |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory text | **150 characters** | 3,000 |
| Headline | **70 characters** | 200 |
| Description | **100 characters** | 300 |

- Introductory text collapses behind **"…see more"** around the 150-character recommendation, and the
  mobile fold is tighter than desktop — put the hook and value in the first ~150.
- Keep the headline ≤70 or it truncates in the banner on desktop.
- Up to 10 emojis are allowed in the introductory text.
- Exact desktop-vs-mobile visible counts aren't published — 150 is the number LinkedIn gives; verify
  in Campaign Manager.

## Calls to action

A preset button set that varies by objective — common ones include Learn More, Sign Up, Register,
Download, Request Demo, Subscribe, Join, Attend, Apply, View Quote. Pick the real next step.

## Register and craft

Professional, credible, value-led — this is a work context, so consumer hard-hype and slang read as
out of place. Open on a business outcome, a stat, or an insight, in the first line before the fold. The
headline states the benefit or offer plainly; the CTA reinforces the action. Emoji sparingly and
professionally, if at all. Personalisation macros (member first name, company) are supported in the
introductory text where you want them.

## Before handing back

- Hook and value in the first ~150 characters of the introductory text; headline ≤70.
- Register is professional and outcome-led, not consumer hype.
- Description reads standalone (it may run off-feed); ad name carries no member-facing copy.
- The CTA names the real next step.
