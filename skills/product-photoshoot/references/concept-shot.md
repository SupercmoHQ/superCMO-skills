# Mode — concept-shot

Surreal, CGI-style premium product imagery — levitating products, frozen splashes, sculptural
arrangements, impossible physics — while keeping crisp, photorealistic product texture.

- **Model:** `nano-banana-2`, or `nano-banana-pro` for maximum surface realism.
- **Pass the product photo** in `reference_images`. One image per variant.

## Brand fit (check first)

For premium DTC, fragrance, beauty, tech, and luxury. If the brand reads warm / rustic / handcrafted /
value, suggest `studio-shot` or `lifestyle-shot` instead — concept will fight that positioning.

## Concept → lighting

Light for the effect. Examples:

- **levitating-suspended** (fragrance) → single soft key from above, 5000K, soft ground shadow under
  the float.
- **splash-frozen-motion** (liquids / beverages) → high-speed multi-flash strobe, 5500K, sharp frozen
  droplets.
- **sculptural-arrangement** (cosmetics) → single dramatic key + grid, 5000K, architectural shadows.

## Prompt template

- `[CONCEPT]` — the surreal CGI scene / impossible setup.
- `[PRODUCT]` — product + branding, with realistic product texture **despite** the surreal context.
- `[COMPOSITION]` — sculptural / floating arrangement; 2–4 clean isolated supporting details, no clutter.
- `[PHYSICS DEFIANCE]` — the specific defiance (suspended droplets, petals mid-air, impossible stacking).
- `[LIGHTING]` — dramatic cool 5000K–5500K, crisp catchlights.
- `[BACKGROUND & ENVIRONMENT]` — clean abstract / geometric infinity space.
- `[MATERIALS & TEXTURES]` — premium surfaces (marble, polished glass, chrome, satin).
- `[LENS & CAMERA]` — `85mm portrait` or `100mm macro`, deep `f/8–f/11` to keep it all sharp.
- `[COLOR PALETTE]` — 2–3 brand tones, high-contrast.
- `[STYLE REFERENCE]` — surreal / conceptual anchors from `style-descriptors.md`.
- `[BRAND INTEGRATION]` — mood aligned to the brand's premium positioning.
- `[QUALITY MARKERS]` + `[AVOID]` — CGI clarity; ban flat lighting, cartoonish styles; Universal +
  Anti-text-warp (if branding shows).

## Composition rules

- Negative space isolates the product for a premium feel (not for text overlay).
- Surreal but never cartoonish; product texture stays photoreal.

## Aim for

1. Composition fully surreal, not half-realistic.
2. Product details sharp and photoreal despite the staging.
3. Scene clean and uncluttered.
4. Lighting crisp with intentional catchlights.
5. Product branding recognizable and unwarped.
