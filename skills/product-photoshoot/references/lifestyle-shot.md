# Mode — lifestyle-shot

The product inside a real-world environment with context, atmosphere, and human signals — a lived-in
story, beyond "product on a table".

- **Model:** `nano-banana-2`, or `nano-banana-pro` when hands or a person are in frame.
- **One image per call.** Wider lens and shallow depth vs the studio look; deliberate asymmetry.

## Scene

Map the product to a natural scene and time of day. Examples:

- **bathroom-vanity** — marble vanity under soft window light (skincare).
- **gym-active** — gym floor with weights, sports context (beverages, activewear).
- plus sunlit-kitchen mornings, a cozy lamp-lit nightstand, an outdoor trail, and so on.

Pick whatever fits the product and brand.

## Prompt template

- `[SCENE]` — environment + time of day ("a sunlit kitchen countertop at 8:00 AM, soft morning rays").
- `[PRODUCT PLACEMENT]` — where it rests, how it interacts with objects; product stays prominent.
- `[HUMAN ELEMENT]` — hands / partial profile, or "no people, traces of presence" for a lived-in feel.
- `[ENVIRONMENT DETAILS]` — 3–5 grounding props that tell a logical story
- `[LIGHTING]` — natural / practical source + Kelvin (eg. "dappled morning light through sheer curtains").
- `[LENS & CAMERA]` — wider documentary lens (`35mm`/`50mm`), shallow `f/1.8–f/2.8` to blur context,
  product tack-sharp.
- `[ATMOSPHERE]` — sensory tone (intimate, nostalgic, energetic).
- `[COLOR PALETTE]` — 2–3 dominant tones; brand hex if given.
- `[STYLE REFERENCE]` — lifestyle / interior / food anchors from `style-descriptors.md`.
- `[QUALITY MARKERS]` + `[AVOID]` — cinematic clarity; Universal + Anti-stock-feel (+ Anti-uncanny if
  hands/people present).

## Composition rules

- The product stays the focal anchor even when small — guide the eye with contrast, light, or shallow DoF.
- Props tell a logical story, not random clutter; deliberate asymmetry and lived-in negative space.
- If hands are rendered, specify finger positions and natural skin texture to avoid deformities.

## Aim for

1. Surrounding objects form a coherent, believable scene.
2. Light reads as realistically motivated, not artificial.
3. The product remains the clear focal point.
4. Any human parts look completely natural (no anatomy errors).
5. Not a flat, over-staged stock look.
6. Color palette aligns with the brand colors given.
